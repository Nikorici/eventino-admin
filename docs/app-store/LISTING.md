## English (U.S.)

**Name** (30): Eventino Admin

**Subtitle** (30): Event sales, tickets and teams

**Promotional text** (170):
Run your events from your phone: live sales, buyers, scanners, vouchers and invoices for every event you organize on Eventino.

**Description** (4000):
Eventino Admin is the organizer app for Eventino. Everything you manage on the Eventino dashboard, now on your phone.

See all your events at a glance
- Total revenue, tickets sold, paid and free tickets, and users across all your events
- Revenue converted to one currency with the National Bank of Moldova rates
- Monthly sales charts with the best day and the change against last month

Run each event
- Live sales, buyers and attendance, with charts by month, week or day
- Approve bank transfer orders, send free tickets and release tickets by hand
- Download the settlement report and the handover document
- Edit the event, its photos, schedule and location, or cancel it

Sales and check-in
- Invite ticket scanners and choose which ticket types each one can scan
- Track every ticket type: sold, remaining and revenue
- Scan statistics per ticket type
- Print name labels at the door from a paired computer
- Pre-sale registrations and the merch store with orders and products

Manage your event
- Ticket types with limits, scan colours, event days and quantity discounts
- Vouchers with usage limits and validity dates
- Private offers with installments, and tables with payment links
- Venue zones, pricing and seat maps
- Event staff and their permissions
- Registration questions, responses and statistics
- Ticket sales invoices, SmartBill and e-Factura

Grow your audience
- Traffic sources and conversion per channel
- Referral links for promoters
- Meta and TikTok pixels
- Email your followers or the holders of chosen ticket types

Eventino Admin is for event organizers with an Eventino account. Sign in with your email code or password.
